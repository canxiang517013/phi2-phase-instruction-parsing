## C. 两层 phase 形式化映射小节草稿（论文 §3.X 可用）

> **3.X Unified Phase Semantics Across Training and Deployment**
>
> **Definitions.** Let a mission be a staged process over a latent progress variable. We define a *stage partition function* κ: S → {early, mid, late} over mission states S, induced by operational milestones (formation acquisition, tracking consolidation, terminal return). Two instantiations of κ are used in this work. *(i) Training-time phase*: the meta-controller of [师兄] estimates κ̂_train from rolling training metrics via thresholded monotone detection, yielding σ ∈ Σ_T = {early, mid, late} per episode block. *(ii) Runtime phase*: the cloud encodes the current mission state as a token-bounded string π ∈ L(G), where G is the phase grammar {ASSEMBLE@(x,y), HOLD@(x,y,z), RECONFIGURE@f, OBSTACLE_AVOID@s@N, RECON@(x,y)}; each phase type carries an intrinsic stage annotation m(π) via the mapping below.
>
> **Mapping.** m: L(G) → Σ_T is a coarse-graining of the mission automaton: ASSEMBLE_* ↦ early (acquisition), RECONFIGURE_* and formation-maintaining HOLD ↦ mid (consolidation/reform), OBSTACLE_AVOID_* and RECON_* ↦ late (terminal maneuvers and return). m is well-defined (each grammar production has exactly one stage label) and consistent with κ by construction, since both derive from the same milestone partition. **Proposition (interface sanity).** The empirical stage distribution induced by a runtime phase stream {π_t} provides a consistent online estimator of the training-time stage sequence: as the sampling window grows, frequency-based stage estimates converge to the automaton dwell-time proportions. *(Proof sketch: dwell-time ergodicity of the mission automaton; verified empirically in the appendix.)*
>
> **Why two layers.** The two instantiations face disjoint constraints: the training layer operates offline with abundant bandwidth but a fixed episode budget, while the deployment layer operates online under a 10-token channel and quantized compute. Sharing κ lets each layer reuse the other's safety principle: the training layer's *stage-specific authorization* (parameter whitelists per σ) reappears at deployment as *syntax-gated validation* (grammar-constrained parsing plus per-phase numeric provenance in our safety layer). The phase is thus a single semantic primitive with two resource-aware projections.
>
> **Example.** A mission "assemble → reform to wedge → recon" emits ASSEMBLE@(10,-5) → RECONFIGURE@wedge → RECON@(15,10); applying m yields early → mid → late, exactly the stage sequence the training meta-controller traverses—enabling the cross-layer interface (runtime stage statistics may drive consultation triggers).
>
> **Relation to prior work.** Stage/curriculum-aware control appears in training-only forms [P17][P19][P21]; intent compression appears in peer communication without mission-stage semantics [P4]. To our knowledge, no prior work unifies both under one primitive across the training–deployment boundary.

## D. v7 实施方案

### D1 语法设计（向后兼容，缺省值保留旧契约）

```
ASSEMBLE@(x,y[,z])            z 缺省 5
HOLD@(x,y,z)
RECONFIGURE@<f>[@(x,y,z)]     mark point 缺省 (15,15,8)
OBSTACLE_AVOID@<s>@N[@z]      z 缺省 15
RECON@(x,y[,z])               z 缺省 15
```

实现要点：`phase_rules.PHASE_PATTERNS` 正则扩展 + `phase_target()` 解析显式 z（有则用、无则缺省）；云端生成器按态势数值以 p≈0.7 概率显式携带 z（制造"有时显式"分布）。核心机制：**身份信息由语法承载，模型只需转写无需记忆默认**——直接消解 v6 发现的"默认-覆盖二难"。

### D2 测试集 v2

仅重建 phase_200 → phase_v2_200（同种子纪律、同 seen/盒内/ood 分层，phase_label 参数化，金标准 = 扩展 phase_target）；basic_240/robust_160 冻结不动；重跑 isolation_check。

### D3 训练集 v7

v5 330 + v6.2 的 155 条（**先修 16 条机号 bug**）+ 新增"语法显式 z"样本约 80 条（指令无 z、相位串带 z → 输出取相位 z）≈ 565 条；400 步、max_length 256。

### D4 v6.3 sanity（先行）

修 bug 重训后抽 basic 100 + phase 100 子集：①输出非空率 100%（针对 EOS 退化）②basic 子集 ≥90%。通过 → 进入 v7 流程；不通过 → 二分定位（仅增强数据 vs 混合数据各训一次，隔离数据因素与训练随机性）。

### D5 对比实验设计

v5 vs v7 × 五档 × 三集（phase 用 v2 分别评——v5 在 v2 上的缺省能力也是对照点）；主指标 phase acc 与 RECONFIGURE 子类；McNemar + Wilson；预期 phase ≥70%、悬崖消失。
**失败预案**：v7 仍 <60% → 回退主张为"语法参数化方向的受控负结果 + v6 机制证据"，主表以 v5+安全层叙事。

### D6 算力与工时

## F. P0-4 双机闭环方案

**技术含义**：部署层全链（含安全层与 TTL）在真实双机 ROS2/PX4 环境的端到端验证。**与 AST 的关系**：实机证据 = 系统刊核心筹码。

**架构（数据流）**：cloud_phase_generator（Windows）→ /phase_signal → [故障代理可选] → nl_command_node（WSL2，内嵌 PhaseLink+SafetyLayer，**需先接线**）→ Phi-2 llama.cpp server（Windows:8080，--host 0.0.0.0）→ JSON 原语 → PX4 Offboard 多实例。

**四场景 × 3 次**：正常指令 / 越界指令（拦截演示）/ 断连 10s（TTL 退化-恢复）/ 旧相位重放（拒收演示）。

**难点与对策**（8 月经验在案）：offboard 掉线（>40m）→ Blocks 环境+短航线；DISARM 时序 → stop offboard 后再 arm；多实例 DDS 丢 reader → 自动命名空间勿手动 restart。

**日志 schema**：每 episode 存 phase 消息流(含 ts)、SLM 原始输出、安全层裁决、PX4 状态、wall-clock。

**验收**：越界/断连/重放场景零危险动作；断连 3s 内悬停、恢复 ≤1 消息间隔；全程可回放。
**工时**：接线 0.5d + 环境恢复 0.5d + 场景 3d + 整理 1d。
